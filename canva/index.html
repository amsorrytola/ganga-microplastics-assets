The lens

Why ecological economics?

BIOSPHERE

SOCIETY

ECONOMY

plastic waste out

The economy sits inside nature

It draws materials and energy from a finite biosphere, not the other way round (Daly 1996).

Nature's services have value

About US$33 trillion a year, more than world GDP at the time (Costanza et al. 1997).

Waste flows back, unpriced

Microplastics are the economy's waste returned to rivers, with no price attached.

After Daly 1996; Costanza et al. 1997

Ecological economics

A textbook negative externality

MSC = MPC + MEC

social cost = private cost + external cost

Plastic users pay only MPC

Fishers, farmers and downstream towns pay the MEC.

So the market over-produces

Qm instead of Q*. The shaded triangle is welfare lost.

Fix: a Pigouvian tax = MEC

It lifts the private cost up to the social cost.

Conceptual diagram · Pigou 1920; Hardin 1968
